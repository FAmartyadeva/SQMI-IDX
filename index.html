This map is centered on SQMI and distinguishes four layers: (1) upstream control through Singapore entities, (2) direct registered holders into SQMI, (3) downstream Indonesian operating subsidiaries, and (4) non-ownership relationships such as China/HK-linked management or technical/advisory links.

  • Stake values are marked-to-market using Rp93/share on 23 Sep 2026.
  • Dashed and dotted lines show important relationships, but not necessarily current ownership control.
